In short. BLATS are the game’s currency for characters, weapon skins and the cases. The game is free to play without them, and nothing bought for BLATS changes a number in combat. BLATS are not money: they cannot be withdrawn, exchanged or moved to another account. They are credited as soon as the payment goes through.

1. Who sells

On the website the payment is taken by the payment provider shown on its page (Xsolla, or Stripe for card payments), under its own terms; the game never sees card details. Inside Telegram BLATS are paid in Telegram Stars, under Telegram’s terms for Stars.

2. What you buy

  • BLATS are a licence to use the game’s currency in your account in BLATKOM ’89. 1 BLATS is priced at 1 US cent on the website; inside Telegram a pack has its price in Stars.
  • BLATS have no cash value: they cannot be cashed out, exchanged for money or other currencies, sold or transferred.
  • What BLATS buy: characters (every character has the same combat numbers), weapon skins (looks only) and the cases. Talons, earned in matches, are never sold for money.

3. Delivery

BLATS are credited to the account you were signed in with automatically, once the payment provider (or Telegram) confirms the payment — usually within seconds. If they haven’t arrived within an hour, write to support (section 1) with the date, the amount and your in-game alias.

4. Cases

The skin case and the crew case are sold for BLATS and give a random item you don’t own yet; the odds are shown before you open one and are applied by the server. They are for adults only (18+), and you confirm your age before each paid opening. Paid cases are restricted in some countries — don’t buy them where they are; the operator may switch them off for a market.

5. Refunds

  • BLATS are digital content delivered at once. By paying you ask for it to be delivered immediately and, where the law allows, you lose the right to withdraw once the BLATS are credited. This does not affect your rights under the law of your country.
  • A payment that went wrong (BLATS not credited, charged twice, a purchase you didn’t make) is refunded on request to support. Telegram Stars are refunded by the game’s bot.
  • When a payment is refunded or charged back, the BLATS it bought are taken back from the balance as far as they are unspent.

6. Your account

Purchases belong to your game account. To keep them when you change devices, link an email (or Telegram) in your profile. If you delete your profile, its BLATS and items are deleted with it.

7. Inside Telegram

Help with a payment in Telegram: send /paysupport to the game’s bot, or write to support (section 1). Telegram support and other bots cannot help with purchases made through the game’s bot.

8. Changes

Prices and packs may change; a change never applies to a purchase already made. When these terms change, we update the revision date on this page.
