Privacy policy

Revised 22 October 2026 · Русский
In short. The game is free to play: nothing in it is sold for money and there are no ads. We keep only what the game, your profile and its statistics need. We never get your phone number, and we don’t keep your Telegram @username or photo. We take no card or crypto-wallet details. Data is not sold or used for advertising. You can delete your profile at any time: Settings → “Delete my data”.

1. Who is responsible for the data

Game operator: [operator name]. Contact: [email or Telegram contact].

2. What data we process

  1. Signing in through Telegram. Telegram passes signed launch data to the game; the server checks the signature. We keep: your numeric Telegram ID (to recognise you next time), your in-game name (built from your Telegram first and last name; you can change it — “✎ Alias” in the menu or the profile) and the language code. We don’t keep your @username, profile photo or the launch data itself.
  2. Playing in a browser without Telegram. Your browser generates a random device identifier and keeps it; the server stores only a keyed hash of it, so a database copy alone cannot be used to sign in as you.
  3. Email sign-in (optional). If you register an email, we keep the address and a hash of your password (scrypt; the password itself is never stored). When you ask for a password reset, the letter with a one-time link (valid for an hour) is sent through our email delivery provider, [Resend, or the provider you configure], which receives your address and the letter.
  4. Signing in with Google, Discord, Facebook, Reddit or Telegram on the website (optional). The provider tells the server who you are; we keep only the provider’s identifier for you (for Telegram — your numeric Telegram ID, the same one the Mini App uses) and when you last signed in with it. Your first name from the provider becomes the starting in-game name (in Latin letters; you can change it). We don’t keep your email address, picture, friends or the provider’s access tokens, and we ask the provider for nothing more than your basic profile.
  5. Game data: the chosen character and profile avatar, match participation (map, place, eliminations, assists, deaths, damage, time), XP, level, achievements, win streaks, time in game, match history; your talon and diamond balances and every change to them, the weapons, characters, equipment and modifications you own, your loadout, and case openings.
  6. Purchases. The game currently sells nothing for money: everything is unlocked with talons earned in matches. If paid items ever return, this policy will say what is recorded about a payment before they do.
  7. Technical data: platform type (for example “Telegram iOS” or “browser”), game version, sign-in times, the days you played, session lengths, app events (launch, connection errors, reconnects) and error reports. Passwords, tokens, email addresses and long identifiers are removed from error reports before they are saved.
  8. Friends and messages: your friend list and friend requests, and the private messages you exchange with friends (text, time, whether it was read). Messages are deleted 180 days after they are sent, and at once with the profile.
  9. Clubs: the club you are in (with your role and the date you joined), the clubs you asked to join, and the name, tag, motto, flag and emblem of a club you founded. Removed when you leave the club, withdraw the request or delete the profile.
  10. Room chat: what you write in a room’s chat (in the lobby, during the match and on the results) the server passes to the players of that room — or only to your team when you write to your team — and keeps nowhere: neither in the database nor in logs. Other players can mute you on their own screen.
  11. Visit statistics: when the game’s website opens, the browser creates a random visitor identifier. We keep it with the first and last visit, the days with visits, the platform type and the domain of the site you came from (not the full address) — to count how many visitors become players. Once you sign in, the identifier is linked to your profile.
  12. IP address is used by the server to handle requests and to protect against abuse (rate limits). It is not stored in the profile database; it may appear in the server’s technical logs (for example, after a rejected sign-in), which are kept for a limited time.
  13. On your device the game keeps a session key, your settings and the random identifiers described above. There are no ad or third-party tracking systems in the game. In the server-less demo (a single HTML file) the alias, XP, level, statistics and practice history are kept only in that browser and sent nowhere; clearing the site’s data in the browser removes them.

3. Why

To sign you in and recognise you; to run online matches, the scoreboard, XP, achievements and the in-game currency; to deliver what you bought; for the profile, statistics and match history; to find and fix bugs and to protect against abuse; and for aggregated statistics (how many people play, which maps are popular, whether players come back) to make the game better.

4. Who sees the data

Other players see your in-game name, avatar, character and results in lobbies and matches, and the players of your room read what you write in its chat. Anyone who knows your in-game name can send you a friend request; your friends see whether you are online, in a lobby or in a match, and only you and the friend you write to see your private messages. Players with at least one finished online match appear in the public Top-100 (name, avatar, level, rating, eliminations, wins). A club’s name, tag, motto, flag, rating and its people (name, avatar, level, role) are seen by all players; join requests are seen only by the club’s head. The game’s administrators work with the statistics panel; every sign-in, look at a player’s card, balance adjustment, password-reset link and data export is written to an audit log. We share data with no one except the hosting provider whose servers run the game and the email provider when you ask for a password reset. Telegram processes data under its own rules; so do Google, Discord, Facebook and Reddit when you sign in with them (the sign-in happens on their page).

6. How to delete your data

In the game: Settings → “Delete my data”. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone: the profile, statistics, match history, achievements, balances and owned items, the email address and password hash, the sign-ins with Google, Discord, Facebook or Reddit, days active and all sign-in sessions are deleted. Diamonds and items you bought are lost with the profile. Usage events and error reports lose their link to you and expire on schedule; payment records stay as accounting records without a link to you. If you open the game again, a new empty profile is created. If you can’t get into the game, write to the operator (section 1) from the same Telegram account or email.

7. Changes

When this policy changes, we update the revision date on this page.
