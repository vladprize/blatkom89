# Asset and rights registry (v0.2.18 part 3)

Everything the game ships or was made from, where it came from and on what terms. The owner wants every
right to the game held by them personally (a future sale to an investor or a company): this file is the
list a buyer's due diligence will ask for. It is kept in the repository next to the code it describes.

**The notice in the game.** The credits screen shows `© 2026 <rights holder>. All rights reserved.` — the
holder is the server's `COPYRIGHT_HOLDER` (e.g. the owner's legal name; until it is set the game's name);
the menu's footer starts with «©». I am not a lawyer: the owner's lawyer should confirm the holder's
wording and the assignments below before a sale.

## 2. The owner's art (`references/`)

69 images supplied by the owner (2026-10 … 2026-11): the poster, character sheets, map layouts, scene
pictures, the skin line, banners — see docs/ART_DIRECTION.md §1 for each. The owner made them with AI image
tools on paid plans. **To keep:** the plans' terms (that the output belongs to the subscriber, and any
commercial-use condition) and proof of the subscription at the time — a buyer will ask. The game uses them
as references and as the source of portraits, hero cards, scene pictures and banners (§1).

Two files are someone else's marks and are **not shipped**: `62-solana-logo.png`, `63-phantom-logo.jpg`
(the crypto banner removed in v0.2.18 part 1).

### Real gun names

Some weapons carried the model names of guns that are registered trademarks of living companies. Earlier
releases already avoided a few (Beretta → «P92», Saiga → «S-12K»). Military designations (PM, TT,
PPSh-41, SVD, RPG-7, M24, M249, M4A1, AKS-74U, «Mukha», GM-94) are generally treated as generic and stay;
the SVD's description no longer says «Dragunov». The owner agreed (2026-11-03) to rename the rest:

| Before | Now (RU / EN) | Whose mark |
|---|---|---|
| АК-47 / AK-47 | Автомат «Дружба» / Druzhba Rifle | Kalashnikov Concern |
| Золотой Калаш / Golden Kalash | Золотая «Дружба» / Golden Druzhba | Kalashnikov Concern |
| Дигл / Deagle | «Бархан» / Barkhan Hand Cannon | Magnum Research (Desert Eagle) |
| Узи / Uzi | «Трещотка» / Rattler | IWI |
| MP5 | ПП «Точка» / Tochka SMG | Heckler & Koch |
| G36 | «Импортная» / Import Rifle | Heckler & Koch |
| AWM | «Дальнобой» / Long Shot | Accuracy International |
| Томми-ган «Чикаго» / Chicago Tommy Gun | «Чикагская машинка» / Chicago Typewriter | Kahr Arms / Auto-Ordnance (Tommy Gun, Thompson) |
| Маузер комиссара / Commissar’s Mauser | Пистолет комиссара / Commissar’s Pistol | Mauser |

Only the names players read changed (`WEAPONS[].name`, `RU_WEAPONS`, the dashboard's names, a few blurbs,
Vityok's «weapons» note — «SMGs» instead of «Uzi»); weapon ids, balance, saves and purchases stay as they
were. `apps/web/src/i18n/names.test.ts` keeps these marks out of the names, blurbs, the characters' notes
and lore, in English and Russian, and out of the dashboard's names. The internal ids (`avtomat`, `uzi`,
`deagle`, `mp5`, `g36`, `awm`, `tommy`, `mauser`) are never shown to players.

## 5. Payments and the rights to sell

BLATS are sold through providers who sell under their own terms: Xsolla on the website (it may act as the
seller of record), Telegram Stars inside Telegram (Telegram's terms for Stars), Stripe as the website's
alternative. The game's own terms of purchase: `apps/web/public/terms.html`, `terms-ru.html` (the operator's
name and contact are placeholders to fill in, as in the privacy policy). See docs/PAYMENTS.md.
